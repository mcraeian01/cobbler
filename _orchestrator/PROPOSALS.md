# Proposals

The orchestrator adds proposed new topics here. The owner approves by changing the status to `approved`, then runs `./new-topic.sh`. Rejected ones stay as a record so they are not proposed again.

```
## <name> · open | approved | rejected
Proposed: YYYY-MM-DD
Goal:
Why now:
Evidence: <topics and what in their state points here>
Would displace:
Suggested tier and priority:
```
